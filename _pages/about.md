Welcome to my page!

I work on distributed systems and applied cryptography: I design protocols and take them all the way from theory to production.
I am currently a technical consultant, working independently and through [Common Prefix](https://www.commonprefix.com/),
where I do research, consulting, and engineering on blockchains, cryptography, and security.

Some highlights of my work:

- **[pod](https://pod.network/):** I co-designed pod, a layer-1 blockchain with optimal latency and censorship resistance.
  Our [paper](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.DISC.2025.4) received the best student paper award at DISC 2025, and pod is now live on mainnet.
- **[Bitcoin Scaling Labs](https://www.bitcoinscalinglabs.com/):** I lead the design and architecture of Bitcoin-IPC,
  which increases Bitcoin's throughput by 23x ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23439), [code](https://github.com/bitcoinscalinglabs)).
- **[Axelar](https://www.axelar.network/):** I co-architected Axelar's next-generation bridging protocol,
  improving cross-chain latency and throughput.

Before that, I briefly worked at [DFNS](https://www.dfns.co/), building on threshold ECDSA and threshold key management protocols,
and at [IBM Research Zurich](https://www.zurich.ibm.com/), building scalable large-scale consensus protocols.

I obtained my PhD at the [Crypto group](https://crypto.unibe.ch/) of the University of Bern,
where I had the privilege to be advised by Prof. [Christian Cachin](https://crypto.unibe.ch/cc/).
My PhD thesis was on [Distributed Protocols with Threshold and General Trust Assumptions](/files/papers/phd_thesis.pdf).

Earlier, I worked as a software engineer at [Entersoft](https://www.entersoft.eu/),
a leading company in the sector of financial technology (aka web2), where
I learned to appreciate high-quality code, good design patterns, and clean architecture.

I obtained my BSc and MSc in computer science from the school of [Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://www.ece.ntua.gr/en)
at [National Technical University of Athens](https://www.ntua.gr/en/).
My master thesis was on network security with deep learning methods at the Netmode Lab.

Concerning ~~hobbits~~hobbies, I enjoy dancing and I teach [Greek folk dances](/folk-dances/).
I regularly watch the Lord of the Rings (and less regularly the first Hobbit film). I am also learning to play the violin.
On a more refreshing side, I sometimes brew tasty beers. Cheers!
